- CONTROL 2, TREATMENT 4. #5 and #9 (both arms): `RUNNER_SETUP_FAILED` (torch pins absent from the index for the image; audit ENV_ROT). #11 and #15 (TREATMENT only): see §7.

- The other direction: 3 TREATMENT BLOCKED verdicts rest on a terminal error that is not the repository's (#3 `17.6` progress fragment; #12 benign TensorFlow warning; #8 apt package the repair added). TREATMENT under-abstains in at least these three. The rate is unaffected (denominator uses CONTROL attribution), but the verdict text on these three is not supported.

## 9. ENV_ROT as a standalone finding

Entries 5 and 9 cannot be installed in the runner at all, in either arm: entry 5 `ResolutionImpossible` during runner setup (class DEP_UNPINNED_CONFLICT), entry 9 `torch==1.2.0` not available (available versions start at 1.11 for this image). 2 of 20 = 10 % of the corpus is unrunnable on a current image regardless of repair. Entry 17 is ENV_ROT by the audit's rule (a torch API removed in later versions) but is REPO-attributed under the registered rules and is in the primary denominator. The repair loop was not entered for #5 and #9 (D-13 in `candidate_v1.3.3_defects.md`), so whether an older interpreter would install them is untested.

## 10. Limitations

- n = 20 (16 in the primary denominator); one corpus, one sealed harness, one repairer model (`nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b`), 3 repair attempts. Absence of recoveries is a result for this harness, not for automated repair in general.
- The corpus is 17/20 without any manifest; the dominant failure is undeclared dependencies, which the repair loop repeatedly cleared and repeatedly met again.
- Recorded spend $21.76; a timed-out operation may be unrecorded (D-8). Entry 13 exceeded the per-entry cap ($5.68); entry 16 too ($2.50).
- Hand labels in §7 are readings of the records by the analyst (this session); they are reproducible from the cited records but not independently reviewed.
- Defects D-1 to D-14 (`candidate_v1.3.3_defects.md`) are recorded, not fixed; a fix requires a new seal and a re-run of both arms.
- The 3-repair limit, the source-patch format and the gate behaviour are design parameters of harness-v1.3.2; a different setting is a different experiment.
